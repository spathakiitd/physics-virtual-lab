Physical Interpretation

Boundary Conditions

The particle is trapped in an infinite well. The potential V(x) = ∞ outside [0, L], forcing Ψ(0) = Ψ(L) = 0.

Energy Quantization

Because the wave must fit exactly within the boundaries, only discrete wavelengths are allowed. Energy is quantized as:

E_n = (n²π²ℏ²) / (2mL²)

Time Evolution

Each eigenstate accumulates a phase over time proportional to its energy: e^{-iE_nt/ℏ}.

In a superposition, states with different energies rotate in the complex plane at different frequencies, causing dynamic interference in the probability density |Ψ|².

Measurement (Collapse)

Clicking "Perform Measurement" collapses the superposition into a single eigenstate n with probability proportional to |c_n|².
