Critical Insight: Compton scattering is not observable in visible light because the photon energy is too low. The wavelength (~500,000 pm) is much larger compared to the electron's Compton wavelength (~2.4 pm), resulting in an extremely small and practically unmeasurable shift.
Critical Insight: High-energy X-rays and Gamma rays have wavelengths similar to the Compton wavelength (~2.4 pm). Thus, the wavelength shift during collision is highly significant, and the electron recoils with substantial energy.
