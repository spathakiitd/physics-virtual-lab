Conceptual Understanding

$$ hf = \phi + KE_{max} $$ $$ eV_0 = hf - \phi \implies V_0 = \frac{hf - \phi}{e} $$

Rules: Emission only occurs if photon frequency $f \ge f_0$. Max kinetic energy is directly proportional to frequency.

Intensity vs Frequency: Intensity increases the number of emitted electrons (current), but does NOT affect their maximum energy. Stopping potential depends ONLY on the light's frequency and the material's work function.

⚖️ Compton Scattering vs Photoelectric

"Compton scattering cannot be observed with visible light because the photon energy is too low to produce a measurable wavelength shift. The change is negligible compared to high-energy photons like X-rays. In contrast, the photoelectric effect depends on overcoming the work function, which can occur in the visible or UV range."
